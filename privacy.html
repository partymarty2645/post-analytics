Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026.

Who uses it. Personal Post Analytics is a private tool used only by its owner, through the owner's own TikTok account.

Data accessed. With the owner's authorisation, the tool reads basic profile information, account statistics (follower, following, like and video counts) and statistics for the owner's own posts (views, likes, comments, shares). It does not access private messages, other people's accounts or any data of other users.

Storage. The data is stored only in a local database on the owner's computer. Access tokens are stored locally and are never transmitted anywhere except to TikTok's own API.

Sharing. No data is sold, shared or disclosed to third parties. No advertising or tracking is used, and this website sets no cookies.

Deletion. The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time in TikTok's app settings and can delete the local database at any time.

Changes. This policy may be updated by updating this page.
